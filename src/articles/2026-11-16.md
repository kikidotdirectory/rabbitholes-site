Since this is about music, I saw it fit to listen to some of their work. Currently listening to Red Line by the band he's apart of, Ozomatli. I find it interesting that it's named after an Aztec god.

Interestingly, all of the external links on his page are broken. His myspace site leads to a 404, as does the link to the official site that's the source of the statement that Abers "is a Pisces." I wonder who wrote that line and what made them think to include it. 

He seems to have had a troubled experience growing up, his father being apart the Revolutionary Communist Party (undetermined if the two are related, though according to the page, the RCP has been denounced for 'being a cult). I'm curious how those things affected Abers as his band[^1] as the band itself was formed from the unsuccessful attempt at a worker's union in LA and is 'known for advocating for promoting various social causes.' I don't know if this is a typo but it reads like they're advocating for others to promote a cause rather than doing it themselves. I will simply choose to believe it as both.

I've now finished listening to a few songs from the band's Youtube and can say that it is most certainly not to my personal taste. The genre list of the band interests me though (at least conceptually): rap rock, chicano rock, funk, hip hop, latin rock, and wold fusion. I haven't the faintest of what most of this list is but the reason it came to mind was one article that stated that Jiro, one of the members, "is the jack of all trades because he can play the Indian percussion *and* timbales and congas"[^2] to which I'm wondering how they all fit together. Given that I still tend to prefer more modern, electronically-produced music, it is unlikely that I will seek this answer.

[^1]: I don't actually know if it's *his* or if he's just a member (assuming the band has a leadership structure) but he is the only individual on the member list with a Wikipedia article.

[^2]: https://www.moderndrummer.com/2007/10/ozomatli/
